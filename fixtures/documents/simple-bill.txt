CHAPTER 123

An Act to amend and reenact § 2.2-4002 of the Code of Virginia, relating to
administrative process; exemptions.

SECTION 1. Definitions.

As used in this chapter, unless the context requires a different meaning:

"Agency" means any authority, instrumentality, officer, board, or council in the
executive branch of state government.

"Basic law" means provisions of the Constitution and statutes of the Commonwealth
which outline the organizational structure and operational mandate.

SECTION 2. Reporting Requirements.

Each agency shall, within 30 days after the effective date of this act, submit to
the Governor and the General Assembly a report detailing compliance measures.

The report shall include:
(a) A list of all existing exemptions claimed under this chapter.
(b) A timeline for implementing any required changes.
(c) An estimate of costs associated with compliance.

SECTION 3. Effective Date.

This act shall become effective on July 1, 2025.
