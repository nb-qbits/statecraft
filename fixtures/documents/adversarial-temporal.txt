CHAPTER 999

An Act to test the refusal behaviour of the legal date grammar.

SECTION 1. Vague Temporal References.

The agency shall submit the report sometime next spring and shall
complete implementation as soon as practicable after receiving
guidance from the Governor.

SECTION 2. Ambiguous Deadlines.

All reviews shall be completed within a reasonable period following
the filing of the initial petition. The department shall provide 30
days notice to affected parties.

SECTION 3. Complex Temporal Logic.

The provisions of this act shall take effect on the first day of the
fourth month following adjournment of the General Assembly.
